85 Seconds to Nothing

The Doomsday Clock is an art piece create by the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists, it counts how close we are to “midnight,” that is, doomsday. Currently, the clock sits at eighty-five seconds to the end times, the closest it has ever been. Why do we care about the clock? It doesn’t show anything useful, it’s virtue signaling, the “oh golly-gee we need to do something!” device, right? That’s what I’ve heard many people say online, the “it’s X seconds to nothing” meme, nothing ever happens, correct?

As I write this, Gaza has been leveled, nothing remains. Iran is being bombed. A madman rules the United States, a quasi-fascist pedophile who wants to do everything to distract from the fact he has touched children. Ukraine has been abandoned. Sudan bleeds. Diamonds, cobalt, rare earth minerals, everything being mined by the hands of African children to power massive data centers that provide nothing useful to this world but pollution and slop. As the old order dies and the reaper comes take its soul, a portal has been opened and from it comes the monsters. I sometimes wonder that, by writing Terraferma, by making mods for Hearts of Iron, I have become the circus, the distraction of the populace.

This is not a text about Donald Trump, or AI, or Netanyahu. It isn’t even about Flávio and Lula, the two men that have been popping up telling me oh-so-dearly to vote for them. It isn’t about how “woke” is supposedly back while India, the US, and many other countries criminalize trans people. This bread-and-circuses that we live in, the Epstein dances on Instagram Reels, the chud memes, the everything, they aren’t the subject of this. It’s the clock, the damn clock.

Why do we care about the Doomsday Clock? Why do I care about the Doomsday Clock? It’s an art piece! It’s not even a clock! Time can go back on it, so why do we bother to check it? The true answer is that I don’t know. There’s a pedophile in the White House, but I care about the clock. The lead between Lula and the man who wants to criminalize my existence is narrowing, but I care about the clock. Countries bleed for the benefits of a few men, but I only care about the clock.

I speak this, I talk all about how much I hate AI, how much I want the clock to move back, but what will I do next? I will feed by work to Claude, Gemini, BingleAI or whatever, just to get a compliment, an analysis, to be treated like an actual writer, I will not protest, I will remain in my room writing. And Claude, Gemini, and Bingle will say “what a fourth wall break!” and I will continue feeding them. Is art, as Kurt said, just a custard pie being thrown from a ladder?

Am I the circus, or am I the one watching?


After It All Burns, Will You Still Be Here?

The world does not care about you. The politicians don’t care about you. If this world is truly dead, if there’s no Montreal for climate change or AI, then what is the point? What’s the point of writing in a world that does not care about art? If we all go back to fire, be that of the forests or that of the bombs, why do we strive to do anything? Looking from Brazil, I wonder when it’s our time to be the next Venezuela or Iran, to be bombed and sequestered, to lose our sovereignty, to become a colony. I wonder this as Americans are not doing anything to stop it. They scream “no kings” but do they mount the guillotines? No, they dance. They dance like clowns.

Me and my girlfriend are hundreds of kilometers apart, yet we both deal with the same woes. Southeast Asia and Latin America seem to be brethren in corruption and people; me and her are in college trying our best to deal with the world. Malaysia still grapples with its past as Brazil seems to have forgotten it.

When this world goes to its grave, after “midnight” comes for either Earth or her systems, be it by bombs or revolution, will I be here to see it? Will we even manage to fix this world in time? I don’t know.

As it all burns, my right side is smiling, my left side is numb, and I can't feel the fire in my face.


Books

I like reading, or at least, that’s what I say. For the better part of the last ten years, I have become someone who doesn’t read, even as I have enrolled into university, I still haven’t been able to read a lot. This is a problem as my major, history, does require me to read a bunch. I do wonder what caused me to lose the will to read, but then I reminisce about the past and wonder: have I ever actually liked reading? I loved books but reading was something else. I always had a book on me, but did I ever open a page? No.

I believe it was the Japanese that created the term tsundoku, or “piling up a bunch of books but never reading them.” I don’t know if my unwillingness to read is because of a lack of time, patience, or both. I am supposed to love books, why can’t I love reading too? What is a historian if they aren’t well-read, goddamit! I write this while I was supposed to be having class, I was supposed to be fostering my intellect, but I’m in a library, surrounded by books I won’t read. I love history, but do I love it enough to force myself to read? I hope so!

I’ll try, at least try, to read a certain number of pages per day. But everyone knows I am a champion of lying about doing something, a champion of delaying my stuff. I want to have the ability my girlfriend has to read, and read, and read. I do not want to be a failure... I don’t want to be more of a failure.

Maybe I’ll manage to be as good as her. Maybe.

Second Choice

What do you do when you are someone’s second choice? You’re not their special person, you’re not their go-to person, you’re their second choice. What do you do if you’re everyone’s second choice? Your best friend has a girlfriend and your girlfriend has a best friend they go to first; not you, never you. What do you do then? Do you ask them to talk with you more? No, because that would be silly. Do you ask them to explain to you why you’re their second choice? No, because that would be silly. Do you cry? Do you cry like the faggot you are? Like the tranny you always were? Do you go to your father, your dearest mother, who you don’t even see as your second choice because they would hurt you more? You don’t. You stay quiet. You bury the thoughts. And the thoughts will not come back. You will make sure they don’t come back. You will be happier as lead replaces your brain tissue. Won’t you?


█████

█████, 14, is a middle-class Brazilian boy. He goes to a rich kid school as his parents sacrifice everything to give him a quality education. He has a couple friends, five of them in fact, they are all girls. He was bullied in the past for being “disgusting” and “filled with germs,” things he has relegated to the dustbin of his past. █████ has always felt something weird, some sort of want, yet he can never put into words what it is. It’s a dastardly feeling that has followed him for years, a primal want. He knows what it is, he knows it is forbidden to be it, that his family would not like it, not one bit. He would be kicked off his home, sent to the streets. █████ knows he can’t do what he wants. He can never wear what he wants, be what he wants. Why must he live like this?

One day, █████ goes to his closest friend and tells her a secret, the deepest, darkest secret (s)he has held. The friend supports her. It’s an addicting feeling, having support, being perceived as what (s)he wants to be perceived as. █████ tells all her friends soon enough, and they all support her. What a feeling! What a rush! Support, for once in (her)his life (s)he feels supported. But this could not last.

One day, █████’s diary is found by his mom. In it, incriminating evidence, smoking guns, the proof of his delusions. Confrontation is necessary. He is cornered. “What is this? What would your dad think?!” An hour goes by. █████ breaks down crying. Another hour, he is drained of his tears. He will be what the family wants for them. But he can’t hide it from his friends, the support is like a drug. With them, she will be happy. But even this could not last.

█████, 15, finds herself in a school party. One of her friends has gotten sick and she offered to stay with her friend until her parents arrive. In the meantime, █████ confesses her love for her friend, which is not reciprocal, but she doesn’t care and continues being friends. Behind her back, her friends plan to kick her out of the friend group. She is too toxic, too vile, too rotten to remain. In one day, she loses her drug, she loses the support. He is forced to go back to his room, with his family, for months. He cannot be himself. But even this could not last.

█████, 16, finds an online community on Discord. He cannot bear to hide anymore, she returns. Emily, 16, is finally treated like she wants. She has her support line again, not in real life, but in this virtual wonderland. She spends hours in front of her computer, she just wants to be happy, and for once, she finally is. But even this could not last.

One day, she finds out her mom saw her Discord and changed her pronouns to “stupid boy.” In shock, she goes tell the school counselor that she wants to die because her mom found out she’s trans. The counselor calms her down and then, the next day, outs her to her father and her mother. Father calls him to talk about her. He does not like what he has to say about her. For days, the two don’t talk. Emily will remain online, but █████ will continue reigning in real life. But even this could not last.

Emily, 17, manages to enter university. She feels safe there, it feels like she could actually be where she would come out of her cocoon again. She is introduced as Emily, her name in their system is Emily, she starts taking the drugs needed to make her happy. She is happy, she is so happy. But even this could not last.

“Is this a joke? Still with this fucking thing, █████? Get away from me.” The mother finds a review she wrote; some kind of divine intervention made her find it in his backpack. She tells everyone in his household about this, and then she threatens to kick her off. When she takes her offer on it, the mother backtracks, says he should remain home, that if he leaves, he will be hunted down by them. A fragile peace descends upon the household in the next couple of days, a damnatio memoriae of what happened is decreed.

But even this will not last.
